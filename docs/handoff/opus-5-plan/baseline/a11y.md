## Top offenders

1. **`color-contrast` (serious) — 116 nodes, every page.** Homepage 37, `/unlock-growth-audit/` 23, `/future-proof-your-seo/` 18, `/services/` 13. The first flagged node on every single page is the same selector (`.xl\:inline`), so a shared component (header/nav region) is one systematic offender, plus page-level muted-text usage. One design-token-level fix likely clears most of the 116. Lens E should identify the exact token/class pairs and required ratios.
2. **`target-size` (serious, WCAG 2.2) — 6 nodes, homepage only.** Touch targets under 24px, e.g. `button[aria-label="Show the Industrial example"]` — the HeroProbe example-toggle buttons.
