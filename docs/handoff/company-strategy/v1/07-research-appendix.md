## C. GEO/AI-search services market

**Demand evidence:**
- **51% of B2B software buyers start purchase research with an AI chatbot** more often than Google (up from 29% Apr 2025); 71% rely on AI chatbots in research; **69% chose a different vendor than planned** on AI guidance. [Hard data] G2 survey n=1,076, Mar 2026 — [Demand Gen Report](https://www.demandgenreport.com/industry-news/news-brief/half-of-b2b-software-buyers-now-start-their-research-with-ai-chatbots-g2/52737/)
- **94% of digital leaders plan to increase AEO/GEO investment in 2026**; enterprises averaged 12% of digital budget on AEO in 2025. [Hard data, vendor-run] Conductor, n=250+ CMO/VP — [source](https://www.conductor.com/academy/state-of-aeo-geo-report/)
- 55% of marketers have GEO dollars budgeted; of those, 70% put it at 11–20% of budget. [Hard data] Scribewise n=205 — [Digiday, Mar 2026](https://digiday.com/marketing/marketers-shift-growing-shares-of-search-spending-to-geo/)
- AI referral visits grew >3× Sept'24→Sept'25 (Similarweb panel) — [source](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/gen-ai-stats/)
- Conversion quality (the measurement-objection killer): Ahrefs' AI-search traffic = 0.5% of visits but **12.1% of signups**; Similarweb: AI referrals convert 7.1%, second only to paid — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/chat-gpt-traffic/)
- Counterweight to respect: ChatGPT drives <0.2% of raw traffic across ~45K sites; a real skeptic camp exists. Sell share-of-voice + conversion quality, never raw traffic.

**Competitive set + pricing:**
- Tool layer (crowded, funded): Profound $99–$399/mo self-serve, $2–5K+/mo enterprise [Primary pricing]; AthenaHQ $295–$2,000+; Scrunch from $250; Otterly $29–$989; Peec ~$29+. Category raised $200–300M+ mid-2025→spring-2026.
- Agencies: every SEO shop bolted GEO on; specialist retainers **$2,000–$10,000/mo mid-market**, premium $8–25K+/mo; **audits $500–$5,000** (the standard land motion: 15–20 "golden prompts" scored across engines). White-label/reseller layer exists at $2–6K/mo resale — a viable wholesale channel.
- Model to study: Lily Ray's practice launched AEO in partnership with Profound (tool+agency bundle); iPullRank/Mike King = the person-led authority play at the top of the market.

**Objections in-market (and the working answers):** "can't measure it" (→ share-of-voice before/after scorecards), "traffic is tiny" (→ conversion quality data above), "too early" (→ 4–8 week surfacing lag + early-mover citation record), "organic is fine" (→ AI Overviews on ~20% of searches with ~58% CTR hit on the top result; ~64% of US searches end without a click — external figures; internal claims bank E1 has its own AIO series with strict phrasing rules — use the claims bank on pages).

## H. Source-quality caveats

Market-size CAGRs (GEO "$1B→$17B", voice-AI "$2.5B→$35B") disagree wildly across research mills — never in copy. ChatGPT-dominance percentages measure different things (referral clicks vs research usage) — don't cite one as settled. The DSG 19.7% ecommerce share is 2022 data. Profound's funding is reported inconsistently ($96M Series C @ $1B vs $55M total). Anything [Weak] stays off pages regardless of how often competitors quote it.
