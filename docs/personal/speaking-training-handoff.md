## Core methodology (already agreed — do not re-litigate, build on it)

1. **Forward-only repair.** Never abandon or restart a sentence. Finish ugly, repair in the NEXT sentence ("let me put that more simply"). Restarts are the disease; fillers are the symptom.
2. **Land-first.** Before speaking, pre-decide only the final sentence (the landing). Route can wobble; destination is fixed. PREP (Point-Reason-Example-Point) as default structure.
3. **Silence over fillers.** Deliberate 1–2s pauses replace "e-e-e". Awkward gaps in week 1 are correct.
4. **Short spoken sentences.** Speak like Hemingway, think like a strategist. Complexity lives in the sequence, not inside sentences. Drill: 8-word sentence cap on complex ideas.
5. **Component library, not speeches.** Well-spoken people do live assembly of pre-built blocks (formulaic sequences). Target: 60–70 blocks over 12 weeks across categories: identity pieces, client stories, explanations, opinions, personal stories, connective tissue (transitions/repairs).
6. **A block saturates at ~30–40 reps** → then it becomes warm-up/benchmark; new material becomes the training load.
7. **Real call > 5 practice takes.** Live at-bats always outrank rehearsal.

## Daily protocol (~2–2.5h, 30 takes/day, video where frictionless)

- 8 takes: new block of the day (Mon/Wed/Fri = sales material; Tue/Thu = identity/client stories; weekend = personal/opinions)
- 8 takes: maintenance rotation (2 takes × 4 older blocks)
- 6 takes: **objection roulette** — draw a real objection blind, respond cold, landing-first
- 4 takes: general cold drills (random topic, zero prep)
- 4 takes: evening re-records of the day's worst moments
- ~20 min review: watch/listen to best + worst take only; write ONE fix for tomorrow
- Quality gate: a take counts only if it had a planned landing and obeyed forward-only
- Phone material: audio-only, standing
- Morning: 15–20 min warm-up (hum, lip trills, 2× 90s PREP reps). Evening: unsaid log (1 thing thought-but-not-said → record the 30s version)

## Scoring methodology for Whisper transcripts (verbatim mode, fillers included)

Primary → secondary metrics:
1. **Restarts per minute** (abandoned/restarted sentences) — #1 metric
2. **Sentence completion rate** (target >90% by week 4)
3. Fillers per minute
4. Landing hit: did the take end on the planned final sentence?
5. Open/close strength; dead-end ratio
Response format: score the transcript, then give exactly ONE drill for tomorrow. Not a list.
Key tell to watch from month 2: cold takes converging with rehearsed takes = skill generalizing.

## Group dynamics system (secondary track, live-fire)

- Master dial (only thing tracked live): "Am I reacting or directing?"
- Rule 1: Finish to the period (same pace through interruptions)
- Rule 2: Hand the floor by name (uses the people-memory work; prestige > dominance — the goal is people going quiet voluntarily, not being out-talked)
- Rule 3: Enter early (first 10 min), land, stop — hold the silence after a point
- Synthesis move = naming what's happening in the conversation ("so really we're all debating X vs Y")
- First-5-minutes openers: logistics grab, named observation, targeted question by name, stake-planting story (sourced from unsaid log), mock-decision. Pre-load 2, improvise 0. One rule per week as focus.
- Post-event log: minutes reacting vs directing, one directed moment, one yielded moment
