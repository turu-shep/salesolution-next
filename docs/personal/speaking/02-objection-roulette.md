# Objection roulette deck

**The drill (6 takes/day):** draw blind. Read the trigger aloud in the prospect's voice. Pre-decide the landing — say it in your head first. Respond cold: forward-only, short sentences, end on the landing, route to the door. Then open [05-objection-library.md](../../strategy/sales/05-objection-library.md) at the card ID, read the canon version aloud twice, and re-record once. The cold rep trains assembly; the canon rep trains the words.

**A take counts only if:** landing planned before speaking · no restarts · ends at the right door · nothing from the [never-drill list](./00-README.md).

**The doors (know them cold):** RE warm → Revenue Leak Audit. IND warm + $5M+ → Growth Call. IND not ready → written diagnostic. Persistent brush-off → EMAIL 1 (one observation, CTA is a question). Under $5M → graceful disqualify, no booking. "Stop calling" → off the list, done.

**Text sparring:** paste any card's trigger into a Claude session and say "play the prospect" — it should push back twice, then hold the line, per the handoff's standing offer.

| Draw | Motion | Landing move | Canon |

| "He's not available." | Both | Binary: "mornings or mid-afternoon?" Assume the callback. | G2 |

### Competitor / incumbent

### Industrial specials
| Draw | Motion | Landing move | Canon |
|---|---|---|---|
| "SEO doesn't work for us. We tried it." | IND | Their failed SEO is *evidence* the old way died, not an objection. The AI answer is a different game. Offer the live check. | IND1 |
| "Amazon's killing us anyway." | IND | Don't deny it — find the opening: specific-part queries where Amazon is third-party junk. "You can't out-Amazon Amazon. You can out-specific them." | IND2 |
