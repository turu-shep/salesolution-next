# Scope & Positioning Rules

**Status:** Agreed 2026-06-12, pending sizing confirmation after 2026-06-14 Ahrefs reset

These are the rules every career-path page, glossary entry, and future addition follows.
When in doubt, the narrower option wins.

---

## 1. In scope

| Tier | What | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **P0 — lead** | GEO Specialist path · Citation Engineer path (term we coin) · AI Search / AEO role definitions · Glossary (AI-search + industrial e-commerce clusters) | Empty SERPs, on-discipline, canonical-source opportunity, LLM-citable format |
| **P1 — supporting** | SEO Specialist path · Content Strategy Specialist path (the two already drafted) — written as the *industrial e-commerce flavor* of these roles | Already promised publicly ("land this quarter"); add internal-link depth; NOT traffic targets |
| **P2 — later, only if data supports** | Adjacent role *definitions* (not full paths): PIM/catalog manager, distributor e-commerce manager | Buyer-side vocabulary; glossary entries first, paths maybe never |

## 2. Out of scope (permanently, unless the strategy changes)

- **Sales career paths.** Off-positioning (we sell AI-search services, not sales careers),
  competitive SERPs owned by Indeed + sales-SaaS, and confusing next to the company name.
- **General marketing career paths** ("digital marketing career path" etc.). Degree-program
  SERPs; zero chance; pure dilution.
- **Courses, certificates, cohorts, gating.** The page's own anti-positioning is right and
  is the differentiator. No email gates — ungated is what earns links and citations.
- **Chasing established career head-terms** as keyword targets in any path.

## 3. The four jobs of every page

1. **Define** — first 1–2 sentences must work as a standalone, quotable definition
   (that's what LLMs lift).
2. **Teach the practitioner** — the path content itself: what to learn, in what order,
   with "test it on your own site" prompts.
3. **Address the buyer** — a dedicated section per role page:
   *What this role does · Signs your distributor business needs one · In-house vs agency
   vs fractional, with honest cost reality.* This is the only revenue-touching surface.
4. **Anchor the vertical** — every example drawn from industrial e-commerce
   ([04-niches.md](04-niches.md)): hydraulics cross-references, MRO part-number long-tail,
   PIM data quality, distributor category architecture.

## 4. Positioning voice

- Written by the operator running client work — keep the existing "not an influencer
  selling a cohort" stance; it is credible and differentiated.
- "The roles that actually exist inside a B2B technical e-commerce team in 2026" stays the
  frame — but soften hard freshness claims in evergreen copy; put the year in examples and
  data, not in promises.
- **Talent stance — DECIDED 2026-06-14: keep "we don't hire from these paths."** The hub
  is a pure authority/citation asset; paths read as operator expertise, not recruiting. No
  freelancer-curriculum reframe, no first-party rates page. (The talent-attraction
  mechanism the evidence supports — Buffer/GitLab/Hook first-party transparency — is
  deliberately not pursued; revisit only if a real hiring need appears.)

## 5. Navigation & site-presence discipline

- Keep current footprint: one nested header item (Resources → Learning Hub), one footer
  link. **Do not** promote to top-level nav — a buyer mid-evaluation should never wonder
  whether this is an agency or a course site.
- Add contextual links *from* guides/services *to* relevant glossary terms (these help),
  not banner-style "check our career paths" CTAs on buyer pages.

## 6. Measurement rules

- **Count:** referring domains to /career-paths/* and /glossary/*; AI citations
  (Ahrefs Brand Radar prompts once quota resets); third-party usage of "citation
  engineering" attributed to us; GSC impressions on definitional queries.
- **Never count:** leads, calls booked, revenue. Junk traffic from job-seekers must be
  segmented out of conversion analytics (exclude /career-paths/* and /glossary/* from
  retargeting pools and goal funnels).
- Prerequisite: reconnect GSC and install web analytics — currently nothing is measured
  at all (verified 2026-06-12).

## 7. Freshness policy

- Every document carries `lastReviewed` (surfaced on the page as "Reviewed <month year>").
- Quarterly review pass over P0 pages; paths reviewed every 6 months minimum.
- A page that can't be maintained gets folded into the glossary or deleted — never left
  to rot. A stale "2026 roles" hub on an AI-currency brand is worse than no hub.

## 8. Investment cap

- Phase 1 (now): publish the two drafted paths (~16–30 h total) + glossary batch 1
  (weighted to the low-KD concept terms).
- **Kill criterion — RESOLVED 2026-06-14, did NOT fire.** Legacy career URLs carry zero
  backlinks (so the redirect-equity case is dead), BUT GEO *concept* demand is substantial
  and partly low-KD (answer engine optimization KD 31, ai visibility KD 25, ai share of
  voice KD 8, geo agency KD 15). So we do **not** shrink to one page. Instead the data
  **reweights** the plan: the **glossary is the lead asset** (real winnable traffic +
  citations); the **career/role pages are citation/entity plays only** (geo specialist 40
  vol, citation engineering 0) — keep them but don't expect traffic.
- Phase 2+: proceed with glossary batches 2–3 ([05 §7](05-glossary.md)); no further data
  gate. Separately, route the **"geo agency" (1,300 vol, KD 15, commercial)** finding to a
  buyer-facing service page outside this hub.
