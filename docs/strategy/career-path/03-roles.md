## 2. Key findings (use these — they're differentiated)

1. **The title is consolidating to plain "GEO Specialist."** Caterpillar's req
   (r0000330321, Oct 2025) was originally "AI SEO / Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
   Specialist," later retitled. Caterpillar simultaneously runs a separate classic SEO
   Specialist req (r0000328865) — **enterprises are splitting the lanes**, which validates
   GEO as its own career path. (Their careers site blocks fetching — quote conservatively,
   link to the listing.)
2. **A publishable salary ladder exists and nobody has assembled it:** GEO $66–118K
   entry/mid, $93–137K mid/senior, $90–165K senior manager, $224–318K director (Jobright
   aggregates); AEO Manager $131–171K + bonus (Citizens). → This is the citable-dataset
   format the strategy calls for. Candidate for the hub's first data artifact.
3. **"Citation engineer" is unclaimed as a job title** (zero postings, verified) while
   "AI citation engineering" has real practice-term usage (Ideapreneur, WLDM, Single
   Grain, Marketing Enigma, GenOptima's "citation outcome engineering"). Coin the role
   openly; cite the practice-term sources.
4. **There are NO entry-level GEO/AEO postings** — every one requires 3–10 yrs prior SEO.
   The honest on-ramp story ("junior SEO/content + AI-search literacy") is itself
   differentiated content; job-board listicles ignore this.
5. **Grainger/Zoro is the best single primary source** for the industrial flavor: four
   roles at one company (SEO Manager, Searchandising Specialist, Digital Merchandising
   Strategy Manager, E-Commerce Copywriter), all tied to catalog/attribute work.
   Accenture's "PIM/DAM Business Analyst | Agentic Commerce" signals product-data roles
   being reframed for AI-agent buying.
6. **Recurring buyer-side hook for every page:** distributors' login-walled pricing/specs
   and aggressive bot protection make them invisible to LLMs regardless of content
   quality. Surfaced independently in the technical-SEO and e-commerce-manager research.
   This is a Sale Solution audit hook.

## 3. P0 — the AI-search lane

### 3.1 GEO Specialist *(anchor page)*

- **Aliases:** AI SEO / GEO Specialist (Caterpillar's original), GEO/AEO Marketing
  Specialist, GEO Manager, GEO Consultant.
- **Demand evidence:** Caterpillar (req r0000330321); Jobright aggregates postings at
  Vanguard, Akamai, Palo Alto Networks, Real Chemistry, Right Side Up, Teleport, J. Jill;
  agency postings at Exalt Growth, AccuraCast (UK); steady Upwork contract flow.
- **Core work:** GEO strategy across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/Claude/AI Overviews; entity
  graph (structured data, internal links, external corroboration); template-level schema;
  prompt-based visibility testing; documenting best practice and educating the org
  (explicit in the Caterpillar posting — a teaching function).
- **By seniority:** Entry barely exists (all postings want 3+ yrs SEO first — the on-ramp
  is junior SEO + self-taught AI-search literacy). Mid ($90–137K): owns execution —
  schema rollouts, entity work, visibility testing across 4–5 engines, monthly reporting.
  Senior ($90–165K mgr / $224–318K dir): enterprise strategy, measurement framework,
  SEO-vs-GEO resourcing.
- **Tools:** Schema/JSON-LD; entity SEO; prompt auditing; visibility platforms (Profound
  ~$499/mo, Peec ~€89/mo, Otterly ~$29/mo, Scrunch, Ahrefs Brand Radar); classic stack
  still required; LLM mechanics (retrieval/chunking, embeddings); llms.txt + AI-crawler
  management.
- **Industrial angle:** make the catalog the citable source — extractable spec tables,
  cross-reference/interchange content ("equivalent to Parker part X"), Product schema
  with GTIN/MPN at SKU scale, AI-crawler access through bot protection.
- **Buyer framing:** in-house only at large scale (Caterpillar/Grainger tier; *$250M+
  speculative*). Everyone else: agency or fractional — work is front-loaded then becomes
  monitoring. Cost anchors: $90–137K mid salary + $30–500/mo tooling vs retainer.

### 3.2 AEO Specialist / Manager

- **Demand evidence:** Citizens Bank "Answer Engine Optimization Manager," $131–171K +
  bonus, 7–10+ yrs, charter to build an AEO Center of Excellence. Also Hawksford
  (GEO/AEO Manager), Veuno, Curate Partners, IRA Financial; ZipRecruiter AEO at $43–48/hr;
  Stackmatix guide: $75–95K specialist, $100–174K manager.
- **Core work:** answer-share strategy; machine-readability standards (Q&A structure,
  concise extractable answers); measurement beyond rankings/clicks; **accuracy/compliance
  governance of what AI says about the brand** — huge in safety-critical industrial
  contexts (pressure ratings, chemical compatibility).
- **Industrial angle:** industrial buying is question-shaped ("NPT vs BSPP," "can I
  substitute brand A's seal kit"). Distributors with application-engineering desks have a
  content moat agencies can't fake.
- **Buyer framing:** don't hire dedicated AEO — postings hybridize (GEO/AEO, SEO/AEO);
  define it on the wiki as a discipline within the AI-search family, bought as part of an
  engagement.

### 3.3 AI SEO / AI Search Specialist *(the bridge title)*

- **Demand evidence:** Caterpillar's original title; IRA Financial "SEO and AI Search
  Specialist"; ZipRecruiter "AI SEO" category; continuous Upwork demand. Semrush survey
  (via Webflow 2026 salary guide): **74% of enterprises plan to hire SEO specialists with
  AI expertise within 12 months.** The field has NOT converged on one title (verified).
- **Core work:** one program for rankings + AI visibility; AI-assisted SEO production at
  scale; blended visibility reporting; stakeholder guidance through AI-Overviews traffic
  loss.
- **By seniority:** entry ≈ junior SEO + AI-tool fluency (~$53K, AI label adds little);
  mid is where the title lives (2–5 yrs, ~$70–117K, AI skills command top of band);
  senior becomes Head of Search owning SEO+GEO+AEO ($120–150K+).
- **Industrial angle:** the single hire that fits a mid-size distributor — keeps category
  pages ranking AND gets the catalog cited. Adds part-number long-tail, supplier-fed
  content dedup, marketplace channel overlap.
- **Buyer framing:** if a distributor hires exactly one search person, it's this hybrid
  ($70–110K). Below ~$50M revenue (*speculative*), fractional/agency wins. Frame the page
  around "one hybrid hire vs agency" — the actual decision.

### 3.4 Citation Engineer *(our coinage — flag-plant page)*

- **Status:** No job postings use the title (verified — SERP wide open). Practice-term
  anchors: "AI citation engineering" (Ideapreneur, WLDM, Marketing Enigma), "AI Citation
  SEO" (Single Grain), "citation authority engineering" (iSimplifyMe), "citation outcome
  engineering" (GenOptima). Supporting stat from those sources: only ~15% of AI Overview
  citations come from Google top-10 — citation is a separate outcome from ranking, which
  justifies naming a separate discipline.
- **Core work (our definition — flag on-page as "how we define the role"):** named
  attribution in AI answers as the KPI; chunk-level content structuring; corroboration
  networks across third-party sources; maintaining citable reference assets (glossaries,
  spec databases, comparison datasets); attribution monitoring and repair.
- **Industrial angle (concrete and ownable):** build THE citable interchange database,
  THE thread-ID guide, THE chemical-compatibility table — distributors sit on this data
  in PIM/ERP and don't publish it in citable form. This is also Sale Solution's own
  playbook for this very hub — the page can say so.
- **Buyer framing:** not a hire — a service definition. "Nobody employs citation
  engineers yet; you buy citation engineering as an outcome." Pricing anchor: agency GEO
  retainers (no salary data exists — flag explicitly).

### 3.5 AI Visibility Analyst *(P1 — function, not yet a title)*

- **Status:** zero standalone in-house postings (verified). Demand is indirect but
  strong: a funded tool category (Profound "popular with Fortune 100," Peec, Otterly,
  Scrunch, Brand Radar), Adobe launched an AEM brand-visibility product, and the duties
  appear inside GEO/AEO postings (Citizens lists Brandlight).
- **Core work:** maintain the prompt panel; track mentions/citations/share-of-voice vs
  competitors; diagnose WHY competitors get cited; flag hallucinations about
  products/specs; feed the optimizer.
- **Entry-accessible** (unlike GEO proper) — realistic first AI-search job, likely as a
  duty inside an analyst role.
- **Industrial angle:** track "best hydraulic hose supplier for ag OEMs"-class prompts;
  distributors are invisible in these answers today — the monitoring deliverable is the
  wake-up-call artifact that sells GEO work.
- **Buyer framing:** never an in-house hire at distributor scale — a $29–499/mo tool +
  hours/month. Wiki angle for buyers: **"this is the audit you should ask for before
  paying anyone for GEO."**

## 4. P1 — established roles, industrial flavor only

### 4.1 SEO Specialist (Industrial E-commerce)

ZipRecruiter avg $67,388 ($53K–90K); Salary.com level II $97K. Caterpillar runs a classic
SEO req parallel to its GEO req; Grainger ~$66K specialist; Zoro posting ties SEO content
to "a substantial portion of annual revenue." What changes at a distributor: the unit of
work is the **template** (one fix × 200K SKUs); part-number queries have zero reported
volume but near-100% intent; manufacturer-supplied descriptions create site-wide dup
content; PIM attribute completeness determines whether facet pages are buildable. Buyer
framing: in-house viable from ~$25–50M online revenue (*speculative*); the classic
mistake is hiring a generic SEO who's never seen a 100K-SKU catalog. Page exists as
"SEO specialist for industrial distributors" — never as generic "how to become an SEO."

### 4.2 Technical SEO Specialist (Catalog Scale)

ZipRecruiter avg ~$81K ($28–57/hr). Distributor sites are technical-SEO worst cases:
faceted nav explosion, punchout/ERP storefronts, replatform risk, and **bot protection
blocking AI crawlers sitewide — zero LLM retrievability regardless of content quality**.
Log-file analysis now includes GPTBot/PerplexityBot/ClaudeBot auditing — technical SEO is
the enforcement arm of GEO; Bing Webmaster Tools newly relevant (Bing powers
Copilot/ChatGPT browsing). Buyer framing: demand is lumpy — buy as project/retainer;
in-house only where an SEO team already exists.

### 4.3 Content Strategist / Industrial Content Marketing Manager

Verbatim posting: "B2B & Industrial Content Marketing Manager" (JobTarget); ZipRecruiter
$45–81K band; senior industrial postings $90–120K+ at 7–10 yrs. The differentiator is
**SME extraction** — the 30-year application engineer's knowledge is the only content
competitors and LLMs can't access. Output skews reference-over-essay (fits the citation
strategy): sizing guides, failure-mode explainers, cross-reference tables. Buyer framing:
first content hire makes sense earlier than first SEO hire (~$45–60K copywriter who
learns the products); honest recommendation for sub-$100M distributors is hybrid —
in-house SME-miner + agency packaging.

## 5. P2 — adjacent roles (definitions/buyer pages, paths later or never)

### 5.1 PIM / Product Data Manager

644 US "Manager, PIM" listings; $42–58K specialist, $70–85K manager, to $181K enterprise.
Platforms postings name: Akeneo, inRiver, Salsify; taxonomy standards ETIM/UNSPSC/GS1.
**In distribution, PIM IS the product** — no normalized attributes → no facet pages (SEO),
no spec tables (AEO), no Product schema (GEO), no agent-readable offers. Accenture's
"PIM/DAM Business Analyst | Agentic Commerce" is the tell. The interchange/cross-reference
data — raw material of citation engineering — lives here. Buyer message: **"fix PIM
staffing before paying for SEO/GEO at scale."** Can't be outsourced (daily supplier/ERP
context).

### 5.2 E-commerce Searchandising / Digital Merchandising Specialist

Anchored in two verbatim Zoro postings (Searchandising Specialist; Digital Merchandising
Strategy Manager): attribute normalization, variant creation, categorization, "SEO
research and publishing" inside a merchandising job. Industrial-distinctive: users search
by part number, competitor part number, abbreviation; a failed search loses a $5K order
over a $4 fitting. **Internal searchandising and external GEO are the same data problem.**
"Searchandising" is a thin-SERP term — glossary entry first, path later. Buyer note: high
zero-results rate → fix this before buying traffic or GEO.

### 5.3 E-commerce Manager (Industrial Distributor)

MSC Industrial "E-Commerce Channel Manager" verbatim; senior distribution digital roles
$140–175K. **This is the hub's primary buyer persona dressed as a career page** — the
person who hires SEO/GEO help. Distinctive problems: sales-rep channel conflict,
contract pricing hiding the catalog from anonymous crawlers (under-discussed GEO
blocker), marketplace cannibalization. Write for buyer value.

### 5.4 Digital Marketing Manager (B2B Industrial)

52,681 US listings; $68–180K band. Often a distributor's only marketer; torn between
trade shows/co-op funds and digital revenue; usually owns the agency relationship. The
page validates that no generalist can also be the GEO specialist and gives them
procurement literacy — "when to call an agency" by construction. **Skippable in v1.**
