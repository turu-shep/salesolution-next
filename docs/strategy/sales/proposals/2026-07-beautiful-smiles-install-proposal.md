# Beautiful Smiles — $30K install proposal (master + presenter notes)

**Status:** call-ready **v4 ("Day-91 bridge")**, 2026-07-07. Client-facing sheet lives as the shareable artifact (screen-share on the call, send after). This file is the master: the frame, the priced foundation stack, the call flow, the same-day rate-letter template, and the claims/sourcing discipline.
**Founder decisions folded in (2026-07-07):**
1. The package is a **foundation layer with two faces — offense and defense** — not a phone-and-follow-up product.
2. The $30K core **includes**: the new Next.js website w/ full compliance build, **30 SEO articles published 10/month across the install**, the **Google Ads foundation** (account in the client's name + tracking + first campaign + creatives, media at cost), the **retention engine** (email + text on their own list), the measurement layer, and 90 days of operation.
3. **Every foundation is itemized and priced** ("bought alone" column) — the buyer can't stipulate value for a layer they never saw itemized. *"How are they gonna value it if we don't tell them the value of it?"* is the operating principle.
4. The **stacked condition ($500K–$1M+) is printed** on the sheet, sourced conservatively.
5. **Nothing held hostage** is a first-class term: site, ad account, content, list, dashboard — in the client's name from day one.
6. **The install must stand alone (v4).** Ongoing/naturally-recurring work lives past the day-90 line: the library growing beyond 30, ongoing ads management and re-aim, deeper reactivation waves + the year-end benefits push, monthly front-desk-score coaching, deeper PMS integrations. The sheet's **"Day 91" section** shows both columns — "yours at day 90, retainer or not" (incl. the **owner's manual**) vs "what the retainer keeps in motion." That section *introduces* the retainer without selling it: the buyer decides at day 90 with evidence, and the number is already in the written rate.

**Note:** "Beautiful Smiles" has no repo file (no precall scan, no audit record) — every `[SLOT]` must be filled from the audit / pre-call findings. Nothing gets invented; a slot you can't fill gets cut, not guessed.

| 5 | **Defense** | WCAG, policy pack, BAAs, texting-consent layer, strictest-state recording disclosures, no pixels on patient pages | **$5,000–$15,000** | Accessibility remediation alone commonly $3–10K+; policy work on top |

| + | **90 days of operation** | Included; one named operator; ends with the **owner's manual** (every sequence, script, setting documented — the system runs without us if they choose) | **$12,000+** | 3 months at the ~$4K/mo internal retainer floor |
| Σ | **Assembled from separate vendors** | 4–5 contracts, nobody accountable, no guarantee anywhere | **$55,000–$120,000** | Sum of rows — vs **$30,000, one name, one guarantee** |

**Anchor discipline:** ranges only, no vendor names, "prevailing market ranges" in the fineprint. These are composites consistent with the architecture's commodity table (§8) and our own published cylinder bands — defensible if challenged, never precise-sounding.

**The two faces** (§2 of the sheet): offense = found first (site + 30 articles + ads), every call answered, every plan chased, list worked, proven monthly, practice worth more at diligence. Defense = WCAG, policies, no pixels on patient pages, consented texting ($500–$1,500/message statutory anchor), strictest-state recording, dispute-proof log, **nothing held hostage**.

**The Day-91 bridge** (§7 of the sheet, between the clock and the options): left column = what stands at day 90 with no retainer (all foundations in their name, 30 articles, sequences running, dashboard reporting, the owner's manual); right column = what the retainer keeps in motion (library growth past 30, campaigns re-aimed at cost, deeper reactivation waves + benefits-reset push, monthly front-desk-score review, quarterly re-aim, deeper PMS integrations, direct line). Closer: retainer figure already in the written rate, month-to-month, report has to beat it, **no decision needed before day 90**. This is the architecture's §6 bridge (retainer decided at day 0, earned by day 90) made visible to the buyer.

**The stacked condition** (§5 of the sheet):
1. Recovered production, 12 months — their audit number ($205K conservative illustration).
2. **Traffic owned instead of rented** — the new row: clicks the articles capture rent for $10–25+ apiece (sourced below).
3. Practice value — 60–85% flow-through × 4–7× buyer multiples → $200K/yr recovered ≈ **$500K–$1.2M** of practice value.
4. Risk taken off the table — five-to-seven-figure incidents avoided.
**Total: $500K–$1M+ against $30,000.**

**Never say "you can't get sued"** — the sheet says "the easy openings close" + carries the not-legal-advice fineprint.

## 3. Claims discipline for this call

- **Allowed:** prospect's own audit/PMS numbers; C-01 (47-hr reply, "LeadSync 2026"); C-04 (5–8% of gross, as guidance); C-05 (as many as 1 in 3, hedged); calculator outputs labeled conservative models; the CPC/search-volume pulls above (own data pull, dated); the 4–7× / 60–85% bands (printed per founder direction, sources on file).
- **One signature away:** 45%/75% case acceptance (Henry Schein One 2026 Catalyst Index — verified; row proposed in `docs/strategy/offer-research/medical-dental-offer-spec.md` §7.1). Sign the C-06 row before the call or stay qualitative.
- **Not allowed:** client proof (none exists); lead-count promises; `lib/stats.ts` ROI stats (industrial-side only); "we'll raise your case acceptance to X%"; incumbent names/prices; lawsuit-immunity promises.

## 4. Call flow (20–25 min, post-audit)

1. **Objectives (0–4).** "What does a good year look like, in production dollars? And what would you stop worrying about if this worked?" Write both; their words return in the rate letter verbatim.
2. **Measures (4–10).** Audit findings: calls rang out, response time, unscheduled-treatment total, overdue recall. Plus the defense scan: "I also checked your website the way a plaintiff's firm would — accessibility, policies, tracking code."
3. **Value (10–16).** Sheet top-to-bottom: leak → two faces → **the six priced foundations (let them sum the column)** → the stack. Then the hinge, verbatim: **"Does that number feel conservative to you?"**
4. **Mechanism (16–20).** The running engine + the 90-day clock. $30K is now an installed asset stack with a punch-list.
5. **Options + fee (20–25).** Three options; Option 1 = the committed $30,000. Exact O2/O3 + month-4 retainer in writing, same day. Guarantee verbatim. Staged billing. Stop talking.

**Pricing notes (internal):**
- O2/O3 = +25% ladder steps (architecture §4); retainers at/above the ~$4K/mo internal floor and bound by the guarantee test.
- **Scope note (v4):** 30 articles at 10/month brings the install to roughly 95–140 solo hours (~$390/h at the year-one $66K engagement; $650+/h once content review + ops are delegated). The library-past-30 line makes the retainer *visibly* worth holding — content is the retainer's tangible compounding asset, per the Day-91 bridge.
- **On the future $45K floor (founder concern, 2026-07-07: "harder to convince; these buyers take credit"):** keep the **published** floor at $30,000 — D3 is signed site-wide, and $30K is the psychological ceiling for a credit-financing buyer. $45K+ happens **per deal, not per price list**, through `max($30K, ~10% of modeled gain)`: "ten percent of the $450K you just called conservative" is a different sentence than a $45K sticker — the buyer authored the numerator. Affordability is a **payment-structure** lever, never a package cut: staged 50/25/25 (live), 100%−5%, and optionally an extended schedule for credit-reliant buyers (e.g., 12 × ~$2,650 ≈ +6% carry) — **[DECIDE: Artur before ever offering; and it's a payment schedule in writing, never called financing].** When scope must give, move items across the day-90 line (the v4 pattern) — don't discount.
- If the audit models **under ~$250–300K/12mo**, the floor fails the 10:1 test — re-scope or decline, don't discount (signed tiering).
