**Anchor discipline:** ranges only, no vendor names, "prevailing market ranges" in the fineprint. Composites that hold up if challenged, never precise-sounding.

**The two faces.** *Grow it* — found first (site + articles + ads), every call answered, every plan chased, the list worked, proven on the report every month, the business worth more at diligence. *Keep it* — accessibility, policies, no third-party pixels on private pages, consented texting, strictest-state recording, a dispute-proof log, **nothing held hostage**.

## 4. Call flow (20–25 min, post-audit)

1. **Objectives (0–4).** "What does a good year look like, in production dollars? And what would you stop worrying about if this worked?" Write both. Their words return in the rate letter verbatim.
2. **Measures (4–10).** Audit findings: `[MEASURED — CALLS]`, `[MEASURED — UNSCHEDULED $]`, `[MEASURED — OVERDUE]`, plus the defense scan `[MEASURED — DEFENSE]`. Only claim what was observed.
3. **Value (10–16).** Sheet top-to-bottom: leak → two faces → the six priced foundations (let them sum the column) → the condition stack. Then the hinge, verbatim: **"Does that number feel conservative to you?"**
4. **Mechanism (16–20).** The running engine and the 60/90 clock. $30,000 is an installed asset stack with a punch-list.
5. **Options + fee (20–25).** Three options; Option 1 is the committed $30,000. Exact Option 2 / Option 3 fees and the month-4 retainer go in writing, same day. Guarantee verbatim. Staged billing. Then stop talking.
