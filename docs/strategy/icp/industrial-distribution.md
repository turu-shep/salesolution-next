# ICP — Industrial Distribution & Technical B2B

**Status:** locked 2026-06-17 (with Artur). Use this for ALL copy on
`/industries/industrial-distribution/`, `/services/*` industrial framing, and
the industrial slices of the homepage.

## Who lands and decides

The **owner / president** of a **$5M–$75M industrial distributor or technical
manufacturer**. Usually not a trained marketer — wears the growth hat among
five others. (A VP Sales/Marketing or the one e-commerce/marketing manager is
the secondary reader; write for the owner, let the FAQ/process sections answer
the practitioner.)

## Two business types (both are the ICP)

- **Multi-brand distributors** — e-commerce sellers carrying hundreds of brands
  and tens of thousands of SKUs. Want to be named over the manufacturer and Amazon.
- **Manufacturers, one brand or many** — makers who want to be found direct, by
  spec and model, and credited for what they build.

## What they sell

The whole industrial catalog — think the range Grainger sells (or could sell),
with **no ceiling**: from a single hydraulic fitting to a tractor. Hydraulics &
fluid power, pneumatics, pumps, bearings & power transmission, motors & drives,
automation & controls, electrical, fasteners, material handling, safety/PPE,
abrasives, welding, machining, HVAC/R, plumbing, test & measurement, fleet &
vehicle parts, raw materials, outdoor power, **agricultural & heavy equipment,
construction equipment**, and on. Their customers are engineers, maintenance,
and procurement who search **by part number, spec, model, and cross-reference**.

## Their language (use these words)

quotes · RFQs · counter sales · line card · "our reps" · "getting found for our
parts" · revenue / sales · "Amazon and the manufacturers going direct."

They measure in **revenue, quotes, and deals** — NOT ARR, pipeline, CTR,
"coverage," or "impressions."

## AI literacy: "somewhat"

They've heard of ChatGPT and have seen Google's AI answers.
- **OK to use:** ChatGPT, Google's AI / Google AI Overviews, "the AI answer."
- **Do NOT use (cold):** schema, GEO, citation share, ERP/PIM, faceted
  navigation, CTR, "coverage." Translate to plain stakes or cut.

## The villain — two sides (Artur's framing)

Almost every owner who calls is stuck on one of two sides (often both):
- **Side one — not enough coming in.** Quieter phone, fewer quotes, slipped on
  Google, Amazon + manufacturers taking business that used to be theirs.
- **Side two — too messed up to handle what they have.** Quotes never chased,
  website is a parts dump nobody can search, can't tell what marketing worked.

**Both trace to the same change:** buyers find and pick parts through Google's
AI and ChatGPT now, and the catalog was built for a web that's already gone.
Lead the page on this, not on an analyst chart.

## Why the AI skips them (the mechanism, in plain terms)

1. Their pages read like everyone's (same manufacturer-supplied copy).
2. The brand looks like the expert (manufacturer's own site is the fullest source).
3. The AI can't read their catalog (quote-only pricing, scattered product data).

## Voice rules

Owner-to-owner, plain, confident, specific. Revenue and quotes over metrics.
Short sentences mixed with long. No buzzwords. Run the humanizer skill on every
new block before publish.

## What they don't care about

Marketing jargon, vanity metrics, "AI SEO" as a buzzword. They want **more
quotes from the right buyers**, and to stop leaking the ones they get.
