## 4. Coordination layer

This is what {{monthly_price}}/mo buys that {{service_count}} separate vendors can't deliver, no matter how good each one is in isolation:

- **Cross-service linking** — editorial feeds the AI Search citations; the website carries the catalog rewrites; outbound references the published authority. The services point at each other on purpose instead of by accident.
- **Shared reporting** — one report against one funnel, not {{service_count}} dashboards that don't reconcile. You see how the services move the number together.
- **One Slack, one owner** — a single accountable operator with direct Slack access. One throat to choke, one person who knows what every service is doing this week.
- **Quarterly cross-service review** — a written review of how the services are compounding, what to double down on, and what to drop or expand.

Five separate vendors optimize their own lane and hand you the integration problem. The coordination layer is the integration.
