## PART 2 — Worked Sample

### Apex Fluid Power — Growth Diagnostic
**Date:** {{date}} · **Prepared for:** {{contact_name}}, VP Marketing

**1. What we heard from your qualifier**
- Shape you leaned toward: Not sure — wanted us to call it
- Services you flagged: AI Search & GEO, Catalog AI, Editorial Authority
- Revenue band: ~$9M ARR
- Current growth spend: ~$18K/mo across an SEO agency, a freelance writer, and a Shopify dev
- Growth headcount: 1 (one marketing generalist, no dedicated owner)

**2. The constraint we'd bet is capping growth right now**
> You're paying three vendors who don't talk to each other, and your one marketing person is spending their week coordinating instead of deciding. The bottleneck isn't any single channel — it's that nobody owns the whole picture, so the SEO agency optimizes pages the writer doesn't reinforce and the Shopify dev ships catalog changes nobody briefed for search.

**3. The shape we'd recommend — and why**
**Shape A — Fractional GTM Engineer, Standard tier ($25K/mo).** In plain terms: you don't need a fourth vendor, you need someone above the vendors. At 1 marketing person and $18K/mo already spent in three disconnected directions, a coordination layer (Shape B) would just bundle the same fragmentation. Shape A puts one operator in your leadership meetings who sets the strategy and owns whether all of it actually moves the number — which is the job your current setup has nobody doing.

**4. Where we'd start — services + sequence**
1. **AI Search & GEO** — your buyers are a hydraulics distributor's buyers: they ask AI tools "best replacement for [part]" before they ever hit Google. We baseline where you're cited (almost certainly nowhere) and fix the pages that decide it.
2. **Catalog AI** — per-SKU rewrites are the same atomic unit that wins both AI answers and product search. Done after the GEO baseline so every rewrite is briefed against what actually gets cited, not guessed.
3. **Editorial Authority** *(phase 2)* — content reinforces the catalog and GEO work once those two are live; starting here first (as your freelance writer effectively does) is why current output isn't compounding.

**5. Rough monthly range + minimum term**
$25K/mo, 6-month minimum, billed monthly in advance. The initial Catalog AI rewrite across your SKU set would be quoted as a separate one-time per-SKU project; ongoing catalog maintenance, plus all strategy and coordination across services, is included in the monthly. This typically replaces — not adds to — your current $18K in vendor spend.

**6. What the first 30 days ship**
- Day 7–14: access to GA4, GSC, Shopify, and your ad accounts; a baseline crawl, an AI-citation snapshot (where you show up across AI answers today), and a funnel map from query to quote request.
- Day 14–30: the first batch of rewritten high-intent product pages live, plus a ranked SKU priority list — a shipped page set, not a strategy deck.

**7. Our call**
- Suggested time: Thursday 2:00pm ET (or reply with a better window)
- The one question we want answered: of your top SKUs, which 20 drive the most revenue today — and which 20 do you *wish* did? That gap tells us where to point the first 30 days.
