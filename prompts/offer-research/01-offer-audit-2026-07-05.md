# Offer audit — every commercial surface (2026-07-05)

What each page actually sells today: prices, guarantees, CTAs, ROI story, install→retainer
framing. Extracted from source by a 5-agent sweep on 2026-07-05. This is the shared ground
truth the offer-research prompts start from.

## The offer ladder as it exists

**Sell-product ladder (hub, industrial pillar, consumer pillar — via `EngagementShapes`):**
`The Revenue Engine — $30K one-time` (~90 days · 3-month engagement) → `Add cylinders —
$4–15K/mo each` → `Full Growth Ownership — from $20K/mo` (3–6 month minimum). Anchor line:
"You already spend $15–40K a month on agencies."

**The contradiction:** the FAQs **on the same pages** (industrial, consumer) sell a different
model — `Sprint $9–35K` / `Operator Retainer $4–15K/mo` / `FGO from $20K/mo` — and the
per-service pages price their own ladders (ai-seo Sprint `$12–24K`, website-dev Build Sprint
`$15–35K`, editorial `$500 trial → $6–14K pack → $4–15K/mo`, catalog per-SKU `$2–7/SKU`,
outbound `$9–14K pilot → $6–14K/mo`). A buyer doing arithmetic across two sections of one
page hits two models. The $30K base appears on **3 surfaces only** — not on the FGO page,
not on `/revenue-engine/`, not on any book-jobs page, not on the homepage.

**Book-jobs model (home-services + medical pillars, dentists niche):** the MODEL is published,
the NUMBER is withheld — "90-day install, one-time fee · 3-month minimum, month-to-month
after · no annual lock-in · exact rate in the audit, in writing, the same day." The roofing
spec carries an unpublished rate card (home FL $2,997/mo + $2,500 setup, CA $3,997 + $3,000;
dental FL $3,997 + $3,000, CA $4,997 + $3,500; media +$997/$1,497) — i.e. ~$11.5–19K first
quarter, **well under the $30K+ core-offer target**. Display of the number is an open
decision (DP-2).

**Day-90 guarantee (book-jobs only), verbatim:** "If the revenue the system brings back
doesn't beat my monthly fee by day 90, I work free until it does."

## Cross-cutting gaps (ranked by conversion impact)

1. **Price ↔ leak-math join is missing everywhere except dentists.** The calculators produce
   $175–400K/yr leaks; the fee is either hidden (book-jobs) or floats unanchored ($30K).
   Joining them IS the 10x story, computed in the buyer's own numbers.
2. **Two contradictory pricing models on the same pages** (base+cylinders vs Sprint/Retainer).
3. **Book-jobs pages show no number at all** — the strong guarantee de-risks an unknown fee;
   buyers can't self-qualify.
4. **Zero proof + the approved stats unused.** The 2.5x/5.2x ROI stats are approved and absent.
5. **No urgency anywhere** — manufactured urgency is (rightly) banned, but legitimate urgency
   (storm season, one-operator install capacity, AI-search land-grab data) is unused.
6. **No named artifact from the primary CTA** on most pages (FGO's "written diagnostic in 24h"
   is the exception) — the CTA asks for a call, not a thing the buyer keeps.
7. **Homepage sells both motions but proves only industrial**; guarantee, pricing terms, and
   calculators are invisible from the front door; hero has no default CTA.
8. **6 of 11 cylinder pages carry no price** and the book-jobs cylinders close with the
   industrial CTA ("Book a Growth Call") instead of the Revenue Leak Audit.
