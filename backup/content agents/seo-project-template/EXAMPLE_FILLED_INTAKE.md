## 1. Client & Business Context

**Client name:** Liori Diamonds
**Website URL:** lioridiamonds.com
**Industry / niche:** Fine jewelry — diamond engagement rings
**Business model:** E-commerce (DTC)
**Physical locations:** NYC showroom (581 5th Ave) + Miami (Sunny Isles)
**Average order value (AOV):** ~$2,500
**Primary revenue source:** Product sales (loose diamonds + complete rings)

### What does this business do?
> Liori Diamonds is an online diamond retailer specializing in lab-grown and natural diamond engagement rings. They offer GIA/IGI-certified stones at 15-25% below Brilliant Earth pricing, with custom rings in 10 business days and 24/7 expert support.

### Who is the target customer?
> Value-conscious couples aged 25-40 shopping for engagement rings. They research extensively online before purchasing. Primarily US-based.
