# rollup-rosters — copy ammo (dated, from the parents' own sites)

**What this is for.** Proof that consolidation pressure is real, recent, and
local, addressed to an owner who is still independent. Every line below is
checkable by the reader in thirty seconds on the acquirer's own website. It is
NOT a threat, and it is not a list of our prospects' competitors to name at
them. The campaign pack cites this file; campaign files are not edited from
here.

All dates and URLs were fetched 2026-08-03 from the parents' own pages
(provenance: `emails/data/raw/rollup-rosters/rosters-2026-08-04.json`). Counts
are derived from the fetched lists below — nothing is carried forward.

## Sentences that survive a fact-check (derived, not asserted)

- **"Singer Industrial has announced 14 acquisitions on its own press page
  since January 2023 — and by its own count closed 10 in 2023 alone."**
  (14 = the dated releases below from 2023-01-01 on; the "10" is Singer's own
  phrasing — HOSER, announced 2024-01-02, is titled "…Marking 10th Acquisition
  of 2023.")
- **"Singer's brand page lists 47 operating companies across 115+ locations."**
  (47 rows on `/brands`, read 2026-08-03; the 115+ locations figure is
  Singer's own claim.)
- **"Motion & Control Enterprises has posted 16 acquisition announcements
  since January 2023 — 17 companies plus three ParkerStore locations."**
  (16 dated announcements below; two of them name two companies each; the
  2024-10-01 item is three store locations bought from MFCP, not a company.)
- **"SunSource's family includes at least 11 named former independents — from
  Ford-Gelatt (2012) to Western Integrated Technologies (January 2024) to
  Dover Hydraulics (May 2026)."** (11 = our one-by-one confirmed floor;
  SunSource publishes no machine-readable roster, so never state its count as
  complete.)
